This project is an application designed to support UMKM in strengthening their competitiveness and expanding their market reach digitally. It was developed as part of the TechFest UI/UX 2025 competition, with a strong focus on delivering an optimal user experience and an appealing user interface.

In this project, I contributed by creating the home page, category, login, cart, and profile page, implementing the carousel feature to present information and content interactively, and selecting the visual assets and also the color palette to ensure the application’s overall appearance aligns with its intended identity. Through this contribution, my team and I aimed to deliver a modern, user-friendly, and effective digital solution to help UMKM grow their businesses more easily.
